Review examines interoception, self-harm, and suicide

A review of 66 studies found that greater trust in bodily signals was associated with fewer suicidal thoughts and behaviors.

Read about the review

The review and meta-analysis was published in Nature Human Behaviour. Read the paper or see how the lab studies brain–body interactions.

20 January 2026 · Publication

Life Gatekeeper trial results published

In a trial across 84 schools in China, teachers who received training reported greater confidence and willingness to intervene.

Read about the trial

Differences were still evident at 12 months. See the trial design, results, and protocol.

2022 news archive

22 November 2022

Zijing Xinqing mental health platform launches

Read the update

YMH Lab developed Tsinghua University's digital mental health public welfare service platform—the 'Zijing Xinqing' mini-program—which was officially launched in November 2022. This platform integrates three core functions: mental health education, psychological assessment, and evidence-based mental training. Additionally, it features a dedicated pandemic support section tailored to groups impacted by COVID-19 (e.g., quarantined individuals, frontline healthcare workers), offering professional mental health services free of charge

22 November 2022

World Health Forum session on inclusive mental health care

Read the update

YMH Lab hosted a breakout session titled “Building Inclusive Mental-Health and Psychiatric-Care Systems for All” during Tsinghua University’s 2nd World Health Forum. Distinguished speakers included Yao Hongwen, Director of China’s National Center for Mental Health; Lu Lin, President of Peking University Sixth Hospital; Joan Marsh, Editor-in-Chief of The Lancet Psychiatry; Yeung Shan, Dean of the Jockey Club School of Public Health and Primary Care at the Chinese University of Hong Kong; and Shekhar Saxena, Professor at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. The forum explored strategies for building more accessible and equitable mental-health and psychiatric-care systems worldwide, with the shared goal of advancing health equity.
