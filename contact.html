The lab’s recruitment page lists postdoctoral fellowships, graduate study, research assistant roles, and research internships. Email the lab to ask which positions are currently open and how to apply.

Research interests and applicant background

The lab welcomes backgrounds in public health, psychiatry, psychology, cognitive neuroscience, computer science, bioinformatics, bioengineering, and related fields. Research includes the mechanisms of mood disorders and suicidal behavior in young people, intervention development using modeling and computing, and research on gender dysphoria in young people.

The lab asks applicants to be familiar with at least one statistical package, such as Stata, MATLAB, R, SAS, or SPSS, and to have strong academic English writing and communication skills. Experience in computational methods or modeling and published work in fields such as psychiatry, psychology, or public health are preferred.
