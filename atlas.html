Projects

  • Cognitive and developmental neuroscience of suicide and non-suicidal self-injury

    Studies brain development and cognitive factors linked to suicide and non-suicidal self-injury.

  • Adolescent Health Enhancing Long-term Plan (A-HELP)

    Prospective cohort study on suicidal thoughts and behaviors among children and adolescents in China.

  • Universal prevention of non-suicidal self-injury

    Develops a school-based program to prevent non-suicidal self-injury.

  • Life Gatekeeper training

    Trains teachers to recognize students at risk and respond.

  • Virtual reality for mental health

    Explores virtual reality interventions for youth mental health.

  • Training for Emotional Adaptation (TEA)

    Develops an online emotion regulation program.

  • Speech-based suicide risk research

    Studies speech-based approaches to assessing suicide risk.

  • Real-time monitoring and intervention

    Evaluates app-based monitoring and support.

Publications

  • Interoception in self-harm and suicide · 2026

    Across 66 studies, greater trust in bodily signals was associated with fewer suicidal thoughts and behaviors.

  • School-based suicide prevention using the gatekeeper programme · 2026

    In an 84-school trial, trained teachers reported greater confidence and willingness to intervene. Differences persisted at 12 months.

  • Brief digital intervention for adolescents with non-suicidal self-injury · 2026

    Text-message support reduced self-injury at four weeks in a trial of 86 adolescents. The difference was no longer statistically significant at eight weeks.

  • Genetic and Neurobiological Mechanisms Underlying Transition in Self-Injury Thoughts and Behaviors During Adolescence · 2026

    Genetic risk and changes in brain structure and function were associated with changes in self-injury thoughts and behaviors during adolescence.

  • Multitrait genome-wide association analysis of non-suicidal self-injury and the polygenetic effects on child psychopathology and brain structures · 2025

    An analysis combining genetic data across related outcomes identified three regions associated with non-suicidal self-injury.

  • Genetic approach uncovering the pathways between childhood maltreatment and suicide attempt · 2025

    Genetic analyses linked childhood maltreatment with suicide attempts and identified several possible pathways through mental health symptoms.

  • Understanding Social Influence of Non-Suicidal Self-Injury: The Interplay of Peer Networks and Adolescent Behavior · 2025

    Adolescents with more friends who self-injured were more likely to report self-injury themselves.

  • School mental health prevention and intervention strategies in China: a scoping review · 2024

    A review of 77 studies found regional gaps and limited coordination between school screening, prevention, and treatment in China.

  • Complex posttraumatic stress disorder (CPTSD) is uniquely linked to suicidality beyond posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD) in adults with childhood maltreatment: A multinational study across four countries · 2024

    Among adults with a history of childhood maltreatment, complex PTSD was associated with more suicidal thoughts or behaviors than PTSD alone.

  • Spontaneous Speech-Based Suicide Risk Detection Using Whisper and Large Language Models · 2024

    The study tested whether combining speech and language features from Mandarin recordings could help identify suicide risk in adolescents.

  • The p factor outweighs the specific internalizing factor in predicting recurrences of adolescent depression · 2024

    A broad measure of mental health symptoms predicted depression recurrence over two years among adolescents in remission.

  • Can digital self-harm relate to suicidal thoughts and behaviors beyond physical self-harm? · 2024

    Digital self-harm was associated with suicidal thoughts and behaviors even after accounting for physical self-harm.

  • Brain structural and functional signatures of multi-generational family history of suicidal behaviors in preadolescent children · 2024

    A history of suicidal behavior across generations was associated with higher risk and differences in brain measures in preadolescents.

  • Effectiveness of a localised and systematically developed gatekeeper training program in preventing suicide among Chinese adolescents · 2023

    Teachers in two early evaluations reported better suicide literacy or intervention skills after Gatekeeper training.

  • Development of the Life Gatekeeper suicide prevention training programme in China: a Delphi study · 2023

    Experts endorsed 201 of 207 proposed statements used to design the Life Gatekeeper training program.

  • Ecological Momentary Assessment and Machine Learning for Predicting Suicidal Ideation Among Sexual and Gender Minority Individuals · 2023

    Daily smartphone reports of mood and stress predicted suicidal thoughts at one month better than baseline measures in this study.

  • Non-suicidal self-injury in Chinese population: a scoping review of prevalence, method, risk factors and preventive interventions · 2023

    The review found many studies of risk factors for self-injury in China and only 12 empirical studies of prevention or intervention.

  • Associations of bullying perpetration and peer victimization subtypes with preadolescent's suicidality, non-suicidal self-injury, neurocognition, and brain development · 2023

    Bullying and peer victimization were associated with self-injury, suicide risk, and differences in cognition and brain measures.

  • A multicomponent digital intervention to promote help-seeking for mental health problems and suicide in sexual and gender diverse young adults: a randomized controlled trial · 2023

    Young adults who received the digital intervention reported stronger intentions to seek help through the three-month follow-up.

  • The transition trajectories of self-injurious thoughts and behaviours among children from a biopsychosocial perspective · 2023

    Changes in self-injury were associated with brain and symptom measures. Family and school support were associated with lower risk.

  • Blind box over-engagement and suicide risk among adolescents and young adults: results of a large-scale survey · 2022

    Buying randomized collectible boxes was associated with higher suicide risk in a survey of more than 73,000 college students.

  • Precision biomarkers for mood disorders based on brain imaging · 2020

    This article discusses how brain imaging could help distinguish different patterns of mood disorders.
